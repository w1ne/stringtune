## How to tune a ukulele in 3 steps

1. Press the microphone button and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
2. Play one open string and let it ring. The tuner shows the note and a needle: left is flat, right is sharp.
3. Turn the tuning peg slowly until the needle stays in the centre. Repeat for every string.

## Tips for tuning a ukulele

- New nylon strings stretch for a few days. Tune often until they settle.
- Pluck with the thumb, not a pick, and let the note ring.
- Tune up to the note: if a string is sharp, loosen it a little below the note and bring it back up.
- Without a microphone, tap a note button under the menu to hear its reference tone and tune by ear.
