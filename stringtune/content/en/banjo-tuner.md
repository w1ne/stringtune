## How to tune a banjo in 3 steps

1. Press the microphone button and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
2. Pick one open string and let it ring. The tuner shows the note and a needle: left is flat, right is sharp.
3. Turn the tuning peg until the needle stays in the centre. Repeat for every string.

## Tips for tuning a banjo

- A banjo is loud and its notes fade quickly. Pick again if the reading disappears.
- Tune up to the note: if a string is sharp, loosen it a little below the note and bring it back up.
- Check the tuning again after a few minutes: new strings and a change in room temperature both move the pitch.
- Without a microphone, tap a note button under the menu to hear its reference tone and tune by ear.
