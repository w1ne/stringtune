The viola is tuned a fifth below the violin: its three highest strings (G D A) are the same notes as the three lowest violin strings.

## How to tune a viola in 3 steps

1. Press the microphone button and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
2. Start with the A string. Bow it or pluck it and let it ring. The tuner shows the note and a needle: left is flat, right is sharp.
3. Turn the peg for large changes and the fine tuner for small ones, until the needle stays in the centre. Then tune D, G and C.

## Tips for tuning a viola

- Hold the phone or laptop close to the instrument, in a quiet room.
- The C string is low (131 Hz). A long, firm bow stroke gives the tuner a clear signal.
- Tune up to the note: if a string is sharp, loosen it a little below the note and bring it back up.
- Does your orchestra tune to A4 = 442 Hz? Tap "A4 = 440 Hz" under the tuner to change the reference pitch.
