## How to tune a bass in 3 steps

1. Press the microphone button and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
2. Pluck one open string firmly and let it ring. The tuner shows the note and a needle: left is flat, right is sharp.
3. Turn the tuning key until the needle stays in the centre. Repeat for every string.

## Tips for tuning a bass

- The low E sounds at only 41 Hz, and phone and laptop microphones hear it poorly. Play the harmonic at the 12th fret: it sounds one octave higher, so the tuner shows E2 instead of E1.
- An electric bass is quiet without an amp. Hold the microphone close to the amp speaker, or to the body of the bass.
- Tune up to the note: if a string is sharp, loosen it a little below the note and bring it back up.
- Without a microphone, tap a note button under the menu to hear its reference tone and tune by ear.
